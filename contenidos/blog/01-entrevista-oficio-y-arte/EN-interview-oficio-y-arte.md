# Real Leather Studio: Over 40 Years of Luxury Leather Craftsmanship in Ubrique

*By [Author] · Reviewed by Jesús Moreno Sánchez, Operations Technician at Real Leather Studio · Published [date]*

**Since 1984, we have been crafting leather goods in Ubrique for some of the world's great luxury houses, including Montblanc, Givenchy and Lanvin. In an interview with Oficio y Arte, our Operations Technician Jesús Moreno Sánchez explains how a family workshop got there, why we pioneered leather golf accessories and how we are passing the craft on to the next generation.**

The interview was conducted by Oficio y Arte, the Organisation of Spanish Artisans (OAE). Jesús is the son of one of our founders. Below, his answers are grouped by topic. Quotes are translated from Spanish. You can also [read the original interview in Oficio y Arte]([ORIGINAL URL]).

> **Real Leather Studio at a glance**
>
> - **Founded:** 1984, in Ubrique (Sierra de Cádiz, Spain).
> - **What we do:** design, development and manufacturing of high-end leather goods and fashion accessories.
> - **Brands we have manufactured for:** Montblanc, Óscar de la Renta, Courrèges, Lanvin, Givenchy, Gretchen, Elio Berhanyer and Devota & Lomba, among others.
> - **Golf:** first collection in 1996; featured at the 32nd Ryder Cup (Valderrama, 1997).
> - **Team:** 25 people, up to 40 in peak season.\*
> - **Exports:** over 80% of production.\*
> - **Catalogue:** more than 300 product references.
> - **Certifications:** ISO 9001 (quality) and ISO 14001 (environment).
>
> \*Figures at the time of the interview.

## Who is Jesús Moreno Sánchez?

Jesús Moreno Sánchez is Operations Technician at Real Leather Studio. He grew up surrounded by leather, in the workshop co-founded by his father, Jesús Moreno Fernández. [Add: years at the company, training and main responsibilities.]

## Since 1984: a Ubrique workshop serving luxury brands

Real Leather Studio was founded in 1984 by three partners with long experience in leather goods. Their goal, Jesús explains, was *"to provide specialised services to fashion and accessories companies by manufacturing their collections of handbags, small leather goods, office items, travel goods and corporate gifts."*

Our confidentiality agreements with major brands are very strict. Even so, over the years we have manufactured for practically every major luxury house, on one-off projects or ongoing ones. Clients include **Montblanc, Óscar de la Renta, Courrèges, Lanvin, Givenchy, Gretchen, Elio Berhanyer and Devota & Lomba**, among others.

Staying at that level in a global market is no easy task: *"Achieving the right level of competitiveness and prestige takes constant creativity and innovation."*

In Jesús's words, we are still *"a family-run SME, artisanal in character but with a business vocation."* That is why we combine handwork with state-of-the-art facilities and auxiliary machinery.

## 1996: the first to bring leather to golf

That search for new directions led us to golf. In 1996, as Jesús Moreno recalls, Real Leather Studio became the first company in the world to design and develop a range of leather goods and accessories for golf. A year later, the collection got its big break at the **32nd Ryder Cup**, held at Valderrama Golf Club (Sotogrande, Cádiz). It was the first Ryder Cup ever played in continental Europe.

Since then, *"we have produced merchandising and corporate gifts for Volvo Klassik, Bentley, the Royal Spanish Golf Federation and the PGA Tour."* We have also made personalised pieces for Easy Golf (Igel-Gesa Müller), Golf Club Am Hockenberg, Hapag-Lloyd and MS Europa Cruises.

Read more in [Real Leather Studio and Golf](/real-leather-studio-and-golf/), or browse our [leather golf bags](/leather-golf-bags/).

## A team of 25 artisans: "a full football team"

At the time of the interview, the team had 25 people with an average age of 35 to 40, *"most of them from Ubrique and familiar with the craft."* In peak season the team grows to 40.

> *"We're a full football team: pattern makers and laser-cutting operators, which we combine with traditional hand cutting; people who paint, others who burnish; stitchers, people who emboss, bench workers and quality control… Although we've refined every role, everyone knows the craft and can step in for anyone else. It's a relative specialisation, because everyone started at the bench, and that makes things easier."*

In Ubrique, leatherwork is a centuries-old tradition, *"and in fact we always talk about artisan-artists."* See how we work in [our workshop](/workshop/) and [our know-how](/know-how/).

> **A glossary of the Ubrique craft**
>
> - **Bench work (*mesa*):** preparing, folding, gluing and assembling pieces by hand. It is the foundation of the craft and where almost every artisan starts.
> - **Burnishing (*lujar*):** smoothing and polishing leather edges so they are sleek and durable.
> - **Embossing (*timbrar*):** heat-stamping logos, initials or marks into the leather.
> - ***Petaquero*:** an artisan who made *petacas* (tobacco pouches), the historical origin of Ubrique's leather industry.

## Keeping the craft alive: from *petaquero* to entrepreneur

For Jesús, preserving that memory is essential: *"We've all smelled leather since we were kids and, consciously or not, we're all captivated by the craft."*

His father, Jesús Moreno Fernández, is a good example. *"He went through every level and role: from petaquero to bench worker to master craftsman; later, from bookkeeper to a particularly innovative entrepreneur. He knows the craft inside out, as well as management and sales."*

That vision allowed the company to keep pace with luxury fashion: *"Shortening lead times, adapting the company to the pace of the big fashion and luxury houses… even today very few workshops dare to do it. It shows that he loves the craft and wants to give it the value it deserves."*

## Why do we export more than 80% of our production?

Because going international has been part of Real Leather Studio from the very beginning. Working for luxury brands is only possible when skills match that market. *"My father built the team little by little, holding the craft in higher regard than was usual. Either way, he was always clear about going international."*

He was a pioneer at trade fairs, first in Spain and later at showrooms in the UK, South Korea, Japan, Australia and the United States. He took a different sample collection to each market, *"because every culture and its customs are different."* Today **we export more than 80% of our production**.

## Training: securing the next generation

Ubrique stands out for its finishes and for the way it folds and stitches leather. *"There are centuries-old techniques passed down from parent to child that a machine will never reproduce… Even so, training is the only guarantee for the future."*

Ubrique has a School of Leather Artisans, promoted by the Association of Fashion Accessories and Leather Companies (**Empiel**). At the time of the interview, the association brought together 51 workshops, including Real Leather Studio, plus two wholesalers and 11 suppliers. However, according to Jesús, the school *"does not guarantee future artisans with the skills required"* for the top segment of the market, which is where we work.

That is why training also happens inside our workshop: *"Young people work side by side with the master craftsmen to secure our future."*

## Raw materials: which leathers we use and where they come from

We want to be transparent about the origin of what we make:

> *"We neither tan nor dye the hides. We work with fully trusted Spanish suppliers, the best on the market, who understand exactly what we need."*

- **80% cowhide.**
- **Goat and sheep leather**, depending on the destination market.
- **Exotic leathers**, such as reptile skins (iguana or python).
- **Unusual commissions**, such as one from Scotland made from water-repellent fish-scale leather.

Find out more about our [raw materials](/raw-material/).

## Quality and exclusivity: more than 300 references

*"We have more than 300 references in our catalogue, supplied by minimum order, with lead times depending on complexity."* Quality control takes time:

> *"We don't take risks. As suppliers to companies bound by certification-agency standards, we have to meet them too. And the pieces carry exclusive logos or brand identity details."*

Today that commitment is certified to **ISO 9001** (quality management) and **ISO 14001** (environmental management).

We don't only work for major brands. *"We also work with designers and private clients. The problem (a blessed problem!) is available stock."* If you have a project of your own, take a look at our [make-to-order service](/make-to-order/).

## Beyond handbags: almost anything can be made in leather

Besides tradition, innovation and exclusivity, *"another of Real Leather Studio's distinguishing values is the experience we've built up in such a select segment."* And the future is wide open:

> *"We're open to other projects. Nowadays most everyday objects, not just desk items but furniture, even kitchen and bathroom pieces, can be made in leather."*

See it in our [leather home accessories](/leather-home-accessories/) and [leather office accessories](/leather-office-accessories/).

## Frequently asked questions

**Where does Real Leather Studio make its leather goods?**
In our workshop in Ubrique (Cádiz, Spain), at Avenida de los Viajantes, 5. We have been making leather goods there since 1984.

**Which brands has Real Leather Studio manufactured for?**
We have manufactured, on one-off or ongoing projects, for houses such as Montblanc, Óscar de la Renta, Courrèges, Lanvin, Givenchy, Gretchen, Elio Berhanyer and Devota & Lomba.

**Does Real Leather Studio tan its own leather?**
No. We neither tan nor dye. We work with trusted Spanish suppliers, and 80% of the leather we use is cowhide.

**Since when have you made leather golf accessories?**
Since 1996. A year later, our collection was featured at the 32nd Ryder Cup at Valderrama.

**Do you make custom pieces for companies, designers or private clients?**
Yes. We produce by minimum order, with lead times that depend on the complexity of the piece. [Tell us about your project](/contact/).
